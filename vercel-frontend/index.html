Modules

Everything in one suite

__smile__

Expression Lab

Happy, sad, angry, surprised… probabilities per face, smoothing over time, live camera or photo upload (group photos supported).
__phone__

Phone Detector

YOLOv4-tiny finds mobile phones in live video or images, raises an alert and keeps a detection log. Tunable sensitivity & input size.
__book__

Study Focus

Attention proxy from head pose, eye closure, gaze, expression and phone use — with timeline, blink/yawn counters and a session report.
__layers__

Age · Gender · Texture

Apparent age (CNN), optional gender guess, LBP/Gabor skin-texture maps, wrinkle-zone metrics and skin-tone (ITA) with a 3D relief view.
__dna__

Bio-Suite

The original Bio-Revive tools: face scans, Phenotypic Age from nine blood biomarkers (Levine 2018), history and PDF report.
__chart__

Dashboard

KPIs, focus timeline, expression mix, bio-age comparison, snapshot gallery and a 3D scene — all from your own sessions.

Remarks

This is a prototype for education and research. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or screen for any condition. Expression, attention, age, gender and skin-texture outputs are statistical estimates from images and can be wrong — especially across different faces, lighting and cameras.

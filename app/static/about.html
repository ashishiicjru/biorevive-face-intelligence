Remarks — please read

This is a research / education prototype — not a medical device

  • No medical use. Nothing here diagnoses, screens for, monitors, treats or predicts any disease, mental-health condition, fatigue disorder, attention disorder or skin condition. Do not use it to make health decisions.
  • Phenotypic Age is a published research equation (Levine et al., 2018) on nine routine blood markers. It is an estimate, not a diagnosis; discuss real blood results with a qualified clinician.
  • Expressions are not feelings. The model reads the visible shape of a face. People express emotion differently, many neutral or concentrating faces are misread as “anger” or “contempt”, and a smile does not mean someone is happy.
  • The study-focus score is a heuristic. It is rule-based from head direction, eyes, gaze, expression and phone presence. It is not trained on learning outcomes and is not a validated measure of attention, effort or learning.
  • Age and gender are guesses from appearance by small, older networks. They are approximate, can be biased across age, skin tone, gender presentation, makeup, glasses and image quality. The gender output is binary and cannot reflect gender identity — it is off by default and should never decide anything about a person.
  • Skin texture / tone numbers are image statistics that change with lighting, camera, compression and makeup. They are not a dermatological assessment, not a Fitzpatrick type, and never used to infer ethnicity, identity or health.

Privacy & data handling

  • Inference runs on your own computer. No cloud AI services are called.
  • Live frames are processed in memory and discarded. Nothing is stored unless you press Save snapshot.
  • Snapshots are JPEGs re-encoded without metadata in data/snapshots; you can delete each one from the Dashboard.
  • Study sessions store only a summary (state percentages, counts, timeline) in the local SQLite database.
  • Camera access needs localhost or HTTPS. If you expose the app on a network, add authentication first — it has none.
  • Download-time network use: model files are fetched once from their public sources (GitHub) and verified by SHA-256. The optional MediaPipe landmarker file comes from Google’s public model bucket unless you install it locally.

Responsible use

  • Use it on yourself or with the informed consent of everyone in view.
  • Do not use it to grade, rank, discipline, hire, monitor employees or students, or surveil anyone.
  • Biometric and face-analysis laws differ by country and region (for example GDPR in the EU and state biometric-privacy laws in the US). Check what applies to you before any real deployment, especially with children.
  • Verify the licences of the upstream models before commercial use (see table below).
  • Treat every output as a hint, show the uncertainty, and keep a human in the loop.

How the attention proxy works

Every frame yields visible cues; a short rule set turns them into a state:

  • Head direction (solvePnP on 6 landmarks) gates the score: turned >~25° or looking down sharply caps it low.
  • Eyes (eye-aspect ratio) → blinks, eye closure, PERCLOS-style drowsiness cue.
  • Gaze (iris position) when iris landmarks exist.
  • Expression contributes only a small weight (negative affect lowers it slightly).
  • Phone in view for >1.5 s multiplies the score by 0.35 and sets “Phone in use”.
  • No face for 3 s → “Away”. Without landmarks the app runs in limited mode and cannot report “Focused”.
